# The gamepad's controls

Read in the app with **H** (core's guide, C6c). The pad draws; the panel on the
right holds its settings, the bar under it says the brush.

## Drawing, on the pad

- **Left stick** — move the brush.
- **A** (Cross), held — stamp, for as long as it is held.
- **L2 / R2**, or the right stick — turn the brush.
- **X** (Square) — smaller; **Y** (Triangle) — larger.
- **D-pad ↑ ↓** — the family before or after; **← →** — the sprite before or after.
- **R3** — the next pattern: single, mirrored, quad.
- **L3** — the brush back to the middle.
- **Start** — record a sequence, and stop: it lands on the timeline.
- **L1** — the brushes panel; **Select** — the gallery.

## On the keyboard

- **Space** or **Enter** — stamp once.
- **C** — clear the canvas.
- **S** — save the canvas as a still: to the vault, and the gallery.
- **G** — the still saved on white, or on nothing.
- **P** — the next pattern.
- **T** — the timeline; **V** — the vault; **E** — print the drawing; **H** — this.

## In the brushes panel (L1)

- **D-pad ↑ ↓** — the families; **← →** — their sprites.
- **A** — take it; **B** — close as it was.

## In the gallery (Select)

- **D-pad ← →** — the saved images.
- **Start** — save the canvas here; **R1** — load the image onto the canvas.
- **X** — download it; **Y** — delete it; **B** — close.

## In the timeline (T)

A recording stopped lands here as a layer. Drag ⠿ to restack — the order is
the blend order; ◉ shows or hides one; S shows that one only, ⇧-click to add
more; ▸ opens its speed, window, filter, blend, opacity and phase. ▶ plays the
stack over the canvas; ■ gives the canvas back.
